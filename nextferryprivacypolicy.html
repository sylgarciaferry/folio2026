Privacy Policy

Next Ferry uses your location to find your nearest wharf, and that location never leaves your device. I don't collect, store or share any personal information.

Who I am

Next Ferry is an iOS app published by Sylvain Garcia, an individual based in Sydney, Australia. In this policy, "I", "me" and "my" refer to Sylvain Garcia.

Location

Next Ferry asks for permission to use your device's location while the app is open. It uses your location for one purpose: working out which wharf you're closest to so it can show your next departure without you tapping anything.

  • Your location is processed on your device only.
  • It is not sent to me, stored on any server, or shared with anyone.
  • The app does not track your location in the background.

You can turn location access off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Next Ferry. Without it, the app can't pick your nearest wharf automatically.

Everything else

Next Ferry has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal data on your device. I don't collect personal information of any kind.

Third parties

Next Ferry contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code, and I do not sell, rent or share data with anyone.

Apple processes the data it needs to deliver the App Store, such as your purchase and download records, under its own privacy policy. If you use iCloud device backups, Apple may back up the app's on-device data as part of your backup, which is governed by your Apple settings and Apple's policies.

Children

Next Ferry is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights and data deletion

Since I hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete. To remove everything the app stores on your device, delete the app.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
