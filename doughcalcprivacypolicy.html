Privacy Policy

Dough Calc does not collect, store or share any personal information. This page explains what that means in practice.

Who I am

Dough Calc is an iOS app made and published by Sylvain Garcia, an individual based in Sydney, Australia. In this policy, "I", "me" and "my" refer to Sylvain Garcia.

Information I collect

None. Dough Calc has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. It does not ask for access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal data on your device.

Anything you enter into the app, such as hydration, starter amounts or recipe settings, stays on your device. It is never sent to me or to anyone else.

Third parties

Dough Calc contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code, and I do not sell, rent or share data with anyone, because I don't have any to share.

Apple processes the data it needs to deliver the App Store, such as your purchase and download records, under its own privacy policy. If you use iCloud device backups, Apple may back up the app's on-device data as part of your backup, which is governed by your Apple settings and Apple's policies.

Children

Dough Calc is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights and data deletion

Since I hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete. To remove everything the app stores on your device, delete the app.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
